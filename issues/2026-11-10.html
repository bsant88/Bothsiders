INNOVATION vs OVERSIGHT

5. Supreme Court Lets Big Bend Border Wall Construction Resume

An unsigned 8-1 order pauses a district judge's ruling while the administration seeks full review.

CONSERVATIVE
LIBERAL
BOTH SIDES
Conservative view
  • Federal law lets the Homeland Security Secretary waive laws to speed barrier construction, and the lower order blocked that authority.
  • The statute limits judges' power to intervene when the government finds swift construction necessary.
  • The Court found the government's interest outweighs the aesthetic, recreational and environmental harms alleged.
  • An 8-1 order, with most justices joining, suggests the legal question is not close.

Positions commonly voiced by the Trump administration, border-security advocates and conservative outlets.

Liberal view
  • The district judge found Congress likely did not authorize waivers this broad.
  • Building through a national park risks lasting damage to the landscape that a later ruling cannot undo.
  • Justice Jackson dissented, and critics say emergency orders skip full review of a major environmental question.
  • Opponents say sweeping waivers let the executive sidestep environmental laws Congress wrote.

Positions commonly voiced by environmental groups, Big Bend-area opponents and Justice Jackson's dissent.
